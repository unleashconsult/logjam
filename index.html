Logjam for Jira

See what's blocking your Jira project, and who to ask about it.

Logjam is an Atlassian Forge app for Jira Cloud. It reads your work items and the links between them, and says what is stuck right now and who to go and ask. It runs entirely on Atlassian's infrastructure and stores nothing.
